## Remaining issues and priority

**No P1/P2 visual issue or required P3 fix is demonstrated by these captures.** The two concrete clarity concerns are addressed in the shown states.

- **Optional P3: profile attachment.** In `profile-expanded-narrow.png`, the panel sits below the avatar as a floating rectangle over the menu. If usability evidence shows ambiguity about ownership, a subtle attachment/continuity cue could help. Current proximity, expanded chevron, and grouping already communicate the relationship adequately. Do not add explanatory copy merely to increase visual detail.
- **Accepted density tradeoff: nested desktop sidebar and saved compact phone Sidebar.** Long host labels, tree indentation, stars, and chevrons compete for space in the desktop navigation; the compact phone rail reduces available text width. Both remain legible in the inspected states, and the primary phone Menu provides a spacious labeled route. I would preserve wrapping and separate action columns rather than truncate labels or force a redesign.
- **No further login-header change is warranted from this evidence.** The link boundaries resolve destination separation while keeping the form high on the phone page. English login-header coverage was not included in the requested 12 English captures, so this is a conclusion about the shown Chinese login state only.
