Student

  • Please use your student ID no. as the username (e.g. 110123456、X1106088).
  • You can please click "Cannot Sign In" to enable your account set the password by yourself.
  • If forgot your password, please click "Cannot Sign In" to reset your password.
  • Or you can please visit the Division of Registration with your student ID for inquiry.

Staff

  • Please use your personnel identification number as the account (e.g. W09090), and click "Cannot Sign In" to enable your account and set the password by yourself.
  • If forgot your password, you can click "Cannot Sign In" to reset your password or contact with the Office of Personnel.

Factory

  • The login account of suppliers is company registered number (VAT number). You can click "Cannot Sign In" to enable your account and set the password by yourself.
  • If you forget the password or have any question, please contact the Division of Cashier.

Alumni

  • Please use your student ID no. as the username (e.g. 110123456、X1106088).
  • If forgot your password, please call the Service Center for the Alumni.

Others

  • Please use "guest" as the account for course selection.
  • Chinese Language Center Students' usernames are the student id no, such as C1101111.
  • Authorization Accounts are like A11111-01. A11111 is the authorizor's staff id no. Authorization accounts are created by the authorizors.

Notice

  • For the security reasons, we will ask you to change password periodically.
  • When you have any sign-in problems, please refer to the "Cannot sign in" document.
